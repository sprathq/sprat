I've gotten into specialty coffee over the last couple of years and started following a bunch of small
independent roasters across Europe on Instagram. A lot of them seem to run their whole operation --
inventory, wholesale orders, subscriptions, green bean sourcing -- through a patchwork of spreadsheets
and generic e-commerce plugins. I want to explore B2B tooling opportunities for small specialty coffee
roasters in the EU and find where there's a real underserved gap worth building for, rather than
starting from a specific product idea.
