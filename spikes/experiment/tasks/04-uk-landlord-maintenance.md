I've got three rental properties in the UK and keeping track of boiler services, gas safety
certificates, and who I called last time something broke is a nightmare of WhatsApp threads and paper
receipts. I want to build maintenance tracking software aimed at UK landlords with somewhere between
one and five properties -- small enough that they don't need or can't afford full property management
software, but big enough that a spreadsheet stops cutting it. I want to know if there's a real business
here or if everyone in this bracket is already fine with what they've got.
