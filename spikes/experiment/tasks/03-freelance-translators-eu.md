I used to freelance as a translator (German-English, mostly legal and technical docs) and I remember
juggling clients, jobs and invoices across a mess of spreadsheets and email threads. I want to build
client and job management software for freelance translators across the EU -- people working solo or
in tiny two-three person collectives, not agencies. There's a lot of existing project-management tools
but none of them seem to get how translation work actually flows: word counts, per-job rates, delivery
deadlines, client-specific glossaries. I want to validate whether this is a real gap before I commit.
