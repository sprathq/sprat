I used to work operations at a mid-sized facilities company and always thought the commercial cleaning
industry ran on a surprising amount of duct tape -- paper checklists, text-message scheduling, disputes
over whether a job was actually done to spec. I want to explore tooling opportunities for US commercial
cleaning companies with roughly ten to a hundred staff -- big enough to have real operational pain,
small enough that they're not already locked into enterprise facilities-management software. I don't
have a specific product picked yet; that's what this research is for.
