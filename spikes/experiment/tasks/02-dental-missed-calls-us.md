My cousin runs a two-chair dental practice outside Austin and she's convinced she's losing patients
because the front desk can't answer the phone during procedures -- calls go to voicemail and people
just call the next practice on the list instead of leaving a message. I want to build something that
recovers those missed calls for independent US dental practices, not the big DSO-owned chains who
already have call centers. I'm thinking some kind of automated callback or text-back flow. I've got
some savings to put into this but I want to know if it's worth building before I write code.
