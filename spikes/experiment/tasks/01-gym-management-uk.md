I run a small independent gym in Leeds and I'm tired of watching bigger chains out-market us with
apps my members actually like. I want to build software for other owner-operated gyms like mine
across the UK -- not the big chain stuff, the ones where the owner is still behind the front desk. The
goal has to be revenue, mine or theirs, this isn't a passion project. I'm picturing something web-first
so I don't have to fight app store review cycles while I figure out what people will actually pay for.
I don't have a dev team yet, just me and whoever I can convince to help part-time.
