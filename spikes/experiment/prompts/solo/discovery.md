an opportunity" workflow end to end by yourself, with live web search. Work through these steps in
order, inside one response:

1. **Intake.** Restate the scope of the opportunity-discovery mission. Work through the questions

   scoring looked good.
6. **Recommendation.** Weigh the scorecard against the counter-evidence and pick the single strongest

   the riskiest remaining assumption.

Do all research and thinking yourself; there is no one else to hand off to or delegate a task to.
