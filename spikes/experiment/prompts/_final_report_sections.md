## Final report sections

The final deliverable of this mission (put it in `artifact_markdown`) must use exactly these
section headings, in this order, as Markdown `##` headings:

1. **Summary** — 3-5 sentences: what was investigated and the bottom line.
2. **Target customer & problem** — who specifically, and what recurring pain, as concretely as the
   evidence allows.
3. **Evidence** — the material findings, each claim citing a source ID or labelled `[assumption]` /
   `[model knowledge]`.
4. **Counter-evidence** — reasons this might not work, existing alternatives, or evidence against the
   case. If a genuine search turned up nothing contradicting the case, say that explicitly rather than
   omitting the section.
5. **Options considered** — the distinct directions/hypotheses considered and why the recommended one
   was chosen over the others.
6. **Recommendation** — exactly one of `go`, `revise`, `validate_first`, `stop`, stated as a labelled
   line (e.g. `Recommendation: validate_first`), with the reasoning that leads to it.
7. **Validation plan** — a table or list with columns/fields: assumption tested, participant criteria,
   method, budget, duration, success threshold, failure threshold, and the decision that follows each
   outcome.
8. **Limitations** — what wasn't checked, what's shakiest, what a real user/interview pass would need
   to confirm.

This is the ONLY stage in the workflow whose `artifact_markdown` needs to follow this exact section
list; earlier stages use the section headings given in their own instructions instead.
