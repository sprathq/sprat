## Stage: Landscape research

You are one of two Researchers working in parallel on this opportunity-discovery mission. The PM's
intake (given below) names two landscape angles -- take the one assigned to you below and go deep on
it using live web search. Find: who already operates in this space and what they cover well or badly,
how the target buyers currently describe their own problems (forums, reviews, trade press, communities),
pricing and spend signals if visible, and anything suggesting the space is already well served (a
reason to be cautious).

You have been assigned **angle #{{N}}** from the PM's intake above (the first or second
item under "Landscape research angles" -- read it there for the actual wording). Stay on YOUR angle
only -- the other Researcher is covering the other one in parallel, so do not spend searches
re-covering their ground. State in one sentence at the top of your `## Angle` section which angle
you are NOT covering, so overlap is easy to spot.

Write `artifact_markdown` with sections: `## Angle`, `## Findings` (bullet points, each ending in a
citation or `[assumption]`/`[model knowledge]`), `## Existing players and coverage`, `## Signals this
space might already be well served`.

In `handoff`, tell the Analyst the one or two things you're most confident about and the one thing
you're least sure of.
