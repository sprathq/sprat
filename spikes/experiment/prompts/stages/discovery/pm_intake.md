## Stage: Intake

You have been given a founder's task brief below, framed as an opportunity-discovery mission rather
than a single idea. Restate the scope in your own words, then work through the questions that would
most change direction: which sub-segment or geography to focus on, what "underserved" would mean here,
constraints, and the intended outcome. There is no user to ask, so for each question, write the most
reasonable assumption given the brief and label it `[assumption]`.

Then choose exactly two landscape research angles for the next stage (two Researchers will each take
one, in parallel) -- pick angles that cover genuinely different ground (e.g. two different
sub-segments, or "who already sells into this space and what they're missing" vs "how buyers in this
space describe their own pain in their own words"). Name each angle in one sentence.

Write `artifact_markdown` with sections: `## Restated scope`, `## Intake questions & assumptions`,
`## Landscape research angles` (exactly two, numbered, each with a one-sentence objective).

In `handoff`, tell the two Researchers, in one sentence each, exactly what you want them to go find.
