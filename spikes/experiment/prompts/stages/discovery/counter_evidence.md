## Stage: Counter-evidence

You have the PM's intake, both landscape memos, and the Analyst's top-3 hypotheses with scorecard
below. Your job is purely adversarial: for each of the top 3 hypotheses, actively search for reasons
it might be wrong -- existing solutions that already cover it well, evidence the pain isn't as severe
or recurring as assumed, reasons the buyer is hard to reach, or anything else that should lower
confidence. Do not soften or skip a hypothesis just because the earlier stages liked it.

Write `artifact_markdown` with one `## Counter-evidence: <hypothesis>` section per top-3 hypothesis,
each with specific findings (cited or labelled `[assumption]`/`[model knowledge]`), plus a closing
`## Net effect on each hypothesis` section saying whether your findings raise or lower confidence in
each, and why.

In `handoff`, tell the Analyst which hypothesis held up best and which took the biggest hit.
