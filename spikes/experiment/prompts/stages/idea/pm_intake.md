## Stage: Intake

You have been given a founder's task brief below. Restate the idea in your own words, then work
through the questions that would most change direction: target customer, geography, constraints
(budget, timeline, team) and the intended outcome (revenue, validated learning, a fundable pitch,
etc). There is no user to ask, so for each question, write the most reasonable assumption given the
brief and label it `[assumption]`.

Then choose exactly two research angles for the next stage (two Researchers will each take one, in
parallel) — pick angles that are genuinely different (e.g. "existing competitor tooling and its
gaps" vs "how the target customer currently handles this workflow without software", or two distinct
customer segments/geographies). Name each angle in one sentence.

Write `artifact_markdown` with these sections: `## Restated idea`, `## Intake questions & assumptions`,
`## Research angles` (exactly two, numbered, each with a one-sentence objective for its Researcher).

In `handoff`, tell the two Researchers, in one sentence each, exactly what you want them to go find.
