## Stage: Recommendation

There is no user in this run to pick between the PM's directions, so you are standing in for them:
pick the single direction you judge strongest given everything gathered so far, and defend it. Then
give your recommendation: exactly one of `go`, `revise`, `validate_first`, `stop`, with the tradeoffs
that led you there. Name the riskiest remaining assumption behind your pick.

Write `artifact_markdown` with sections: `## Direction selected` (name it and say why, referencing the
PM's directions), `## Recommendation` (state it as `Recommendation: <go|revise|validate_first|stop>`,
then justify it), `## Riskiest remaining assumption`, `## What would change this recommendation`.

In `handoff`, tell the PM exactly what the brief needs to nail for this direction to be usable.
