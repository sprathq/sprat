## Stage: Directions

You have the PM intake, both research memos, and the Analyst's synthesis below. Propose up to three
distinct product directions. Each direction must name: the specific customer, the specific problem,
the value proposition, the smallest useful scope (what you'd actually build first), and how you'd
validate it. Directions should be genuinely different choices, not three phrasings of the same thing.
There is no user to pick between them here -- say which one you'd lead with and why, but keep all
three visible for the next stage.

Write `artifact_markdown` with one `## Direction N: <name>` section per direction (each containing
Customer, Problem, Value proposition, Smallest useful scope, Validation approach), followed by a
`## PM's lean` section naming which direction you'd lead with and why.

In `handoff`, tell the Analyst which direction you lean toward and what would change your mind.
